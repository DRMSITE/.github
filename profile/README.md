## DRMSITE LTD

Small web applications for communities and for individuals.

- **[ParishMap](https://github.com/DRMSITE/parishmap)**: a private map and project board for
  parish, town and community councils. Free and open source.
- **[Traffic Light Timer](https://traffic-light-timer.co.uk)**: a full-screen countdown for
  meetings and talks that goes green, amber, then red.

The rest of what we make is at [drmsite.com](https://drmsite.com).
